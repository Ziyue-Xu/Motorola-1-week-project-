We are MOTO-MED

One of the biggest issues in health centers/hospitals is the chaos of the current triage system. Thousands of people die waiting for beds in hospitals, during instances such as covid, the inaccuracy and inefficiency of our current system becomes more apparent.

Our product has been designed to alleviate the stress of not only the Healthcare workers; but to improve customer satisfaction. 79% of nurses have reported that their units are inadequately staffed. This is where our moto-pad comes in. When the patient walks in, the first thing they see is moto-pad, our specialized tablet with moto-med AI. Here, they annotate their symptoms and explain their pain level. With all this information, our AI will collect that data along with the patients medical history. Then, the AI will list the labs needed to be performed and will grab an intake of your problem list and automatically assign you to a number in line determined by acuity of problem. Once the labs needed to be performed are decided, the doctor will need to confirm this information. Finally, once all that information is gathered, the doctor will need to see the patient to give the final diagnosis and medicate if needed. This system reduces wait time significantly and makes sure that all the patients are given the care they need.
